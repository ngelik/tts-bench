# Tiny Voca community benchmark coverage

The [upstream directory at 4bf796d](https://github.com/n4ze3m/tiny-voca/blob/4bf796d29ea775edd5a7cc5ee3a6806b3c3b91f6/README.md#community) lists 42 language-and-voice entries. Compared with the original 34-entry campaign, eight entries were added and Bengali Voice expanded into Browser Voice. The [dashboard at acaa9e8](https://github.com/ngelik/tts-bench/tree/acaa9e82efdf8bd937050a838ba9e63652e1b2ec/results) has full-article measurements for 33 of the original entries. Kurdish Behdînî remains a [hosted demo](https://behdinan.com/) with no reference browser result.

Seven spoken languages in the expanded directory lack a measured board: Uzbek, Mandarin Chinese, Gujarati, Telugu, Sinhala, Persian and Luganda. European Portuguese also lacks a measured result. The published Portuguese results use Brazilian Portuguese. Hebrew, Hindi and Malayalam already have boards, but the newly listed models or language routes have no matching receipts.

The Browser Voice publisher serves matching compact weights, an acoustic graph, a pronunciation lexicon and two G2P graphs for five released variants. All 25 deployed files matched the corresponding pinned model-repository bytes. The publisher also lists an English Nivedita release. Telugu Sreekar is a distinct INT8 preview. The deployed manifest links a 967,398-byte weights file, an acoustic graph and both pronunciation graphs. Native and Roman Telugu can be evaluated from that preview once the article editions and adapter are ready. The four Indian-language Roman converters use the publisher’s Dakshina-derived data under CC BY-SA 4.0. [Live model manifest](https://tts.hansda.in/languages.json) · [Publisher credits](https://tts.hansda.in/credits.html)

The intake above records coverage at the cited revision. Each published score requires its own full-article receipt, a verified effective backend and the registered fixture hash. Roman input and regional editions retain separate fixtures and measurements.
