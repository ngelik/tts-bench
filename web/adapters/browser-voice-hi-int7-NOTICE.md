# Browser Voice Hindi INT7 notice

This profile selects Tamal Hansda's Hindi Voice Compact INT7 checkpoint and the fixed Ishwari voice. It accepts Devanagari from `hi-article-v1` and Roman Hindi from `hi-latn-article-v1`. The checkpoint revision is `04e39413827f3d45af54ceb480cb77e732983c1b`; the browser worker source is pinned separately at `f2977727b8baccb989f80b492ea8da00124ecba3`. Asset URLs, byte lengths and SHA-256 digests, including both G2P graphs and the immutable credits file, are in `assets.lock.json`.

The checkpoint declares Apache-2.0. The 20-hour synthetic speech and original release annotations are CC BY 4.0; source prompts retain their original terms. The pinned dataset card identifies the Common Voice Hindi Sentence Collector prompt file (CC0-1.0), the Indic TTS Mini `hi_F` and `hi_M` prompt indexes (CC BY 4.0), and credits Mozilla Common Voice contributors, AI4Bharat and Rasa contributors, and the indic-tts-mini-24k index publisher. It also cites Maya 2 Native (Apache-2.0) and Kyutai Mimi (CC BY 4.0). Keep source-prompt terms separate from the checkpoint license; no blanket license for generated recordings is inferred.

The pinned publisher credits identify Tiny Voca by Muhammed Nazeem under Apache-2.0, Maya 2 Native, Kyutai Mimi (CC BY 4.0), eSpeak NG pronunciation labels and Dakshina lexicons for Roman-input converters. For Roman input the stock worker loads its original Hindi dictionary, adapted from the Google Research Dakshina training lexicons under CC BY-SA 4.0. [Dakshina data and attribution](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/dakshina) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) ONNX Runtime Web 1.24.3 is MIT. At UI speed 1 the stock browser uses `paceScale` 0.9333333333333333. The worker uses one-thread WASM for acoustic and G2P inference. The reported 934,704 scalar values cover the 200 external INT7 acoustic graph initializers and exclude the separate G2P encoder and decoder. No speech-quality or benchmark claim is made by this source profile.
