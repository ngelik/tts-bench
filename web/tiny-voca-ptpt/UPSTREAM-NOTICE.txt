Changes from the original (see the git history from commit 5f645b9):
- src/tiny_voca/text.py, config.py, model.py: European Portuguese front end
  (TINY_VOCA_FRONTEND=pt) and its phoneme inventory.
- src/tiny_voca/align.py, annotate.py: alignment accepts the phoneme
  recogniser's labels for each Portuguese phoneme.
- src/tiny_voca/infer.py: the front end is matched to the checkpoint without
  switching inventories after import.
- pronuncia-pt/: new, a rule-based European Portuguese phonemizer.
- pt-pt/: new, the data generation and training scripts of this model.

The training data was generated with Sopro (samuel-vitorino/sopro-v2-turbo,
Apache-2.0) and Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign (Apache-2.0) from Common Voice
sentences (CC0).
