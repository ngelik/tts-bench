# Alpa-es source and notices

Francisco Carlos Erra adapted Alpa by 77ethers and tiny-voca by Muhammed
Nazeem. This profile pins both model and source at Hugging Face revision
`de2bf894bdd9eb768ae03fc6b385176a0ff505d1`. `frontend.mjs` ports its
`alpa/text.py` to JavaScript. This directory retains the exact upstream
Apache-2.0 license and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
[Pinned checkpoint and source](https://huggingface.co/FranciscoCarlosErra/alpa-es/tree/de2bf894bdd9eb768ae03fc6b385176a0ff505d1).

Alpa-es was trained on OpenSLR 61 recordings of Argentinian Spanish
(CC BY-SA 4.0), credited to Guevara-Rukoz et al. The model card asks users
to check how the dataset's share-alike terms apply to weight use.
[OpenSLR 61](https://www.openslr.org/61/) ·
[Pinned model card](https://huggingface.co/FranciscoCarlosErra/alpa-es/blob/de2bf894bdd9eb768ae03fc6b385176a0ff505d1/README.md).
