Leila ne recorder ke paas headphone rakhe aur Jo purani tasveer ghar le gayi taaki use adhik uchcha resolution mein scan kar sake. Kamaraa dhire-dhire khali huaa. Baahar, nauka ki dek-laaiten andhere kapare mein khinche gae dhaage ki tarah khaari par chal rahi thin.

Company ne teen sharten maan lin aur rakharakhaav ki sharta ko chunauti di. Baatachit November tak chalati rahi. Koi nahi bataa sakta tha ki pariyojana vasant tak banegi yaa restaraan apni pehli sardi jhel paaegaa. Kaagaz par dweep ko ek surakshit reling mili thi, lakari yaa ispaat mein abhi nahi. Aapatkalin radio ki ghari ab bhi samay kho deti thi aur backup generator ko ab bhi nai battery chahiye thi. Mara ka anubandh tis November ko khatma huaa. Jaane se pehle usne abhilekhagar ki kaamakaaji suchi do drive mein kopi ki, ek prati June ke lie chhaapi aur satyaapan ki tareekh ek chhote card par likhi. Donon pratiyon ko pankti-dar-pankti milaakar jaanchaa gaya. Usne saakshaatkaaron ki koi niji prati nahi rakhi. Har vaktaa ki pasand recording ke saath judi rahi, use chalaane wale computer ke saath nahi.

Apni aakhiri subah Mara akeli uttari chabootre tak gayi. Jwar kam tha aur purani seedhiyan paani tak saaf dikhai de rahi thin. Kisi ne kichar wale khali hisse par ek chhota takhtaa rakhaa tha aur uska ek siraa peela rang diya tha. Woh takhtaa manzuurashudaa marammat nahi tha. Woh ek asthayi pul tha - itanaa chauda ki thailaa lie saavadhaani se chalane wala ek vyakti use paar kar sake. Mara ne uski tasveer li, jagah darja ki aur nagar ki rakharakhaav toli ke lie not joraa. Pustakalay ki or se use bandargah ki ghanti sunai di, jabaki vahaan uski rassi khinchane wala koi nahi tha. Havaa mein woh ek baar baji - dhaatu ki dhili-si awaaz, jo sanket bhi ho sakti thi yaa bas hilati hui koi purani chiz. Usne apni notebook mein donon sambhaavanaaen likhin.

Abhilekhagar ne gayab naam vaapas nahi khojaa, prakaashastambh phir se nahi kholaa aur dweep ka bhavishya tay nahi kiya. Usne sarvajanik roop se savaal puuchhanaa aasaan aur andaaze se unakaa javaab denaa kathin bana diya. Aarekh, bahi, sakshatkar aur Nia ka nilaa naksha ab alag-alag jagahon par rakhe the; har ek ke saath yah darja tha ki woh kahaan se aaya aur use kaun dekh sakta tha. June pustakalay mein kaagazi suchi rakhati thin. Leila apne recorder ke paas sahamati ki suchi rakhati thi. Theo ne radio ki battery par nayaa lebal lagaayaa: har mahine jaanchen. Jo ne tasveer ko "uttari chabootra, unnis sau chauranve se pehle" likhe aavaran mein rakh diya. Mausam anumati detaa to nauka ab bhi saat bajkar das minute par paar jati. Aur jab ghanti do baar bajati, log oonchi zameen ki or dekhate, phir ek-doosre ki or - yah dekhane ke lie ki use aur kisane sunaa tha.
