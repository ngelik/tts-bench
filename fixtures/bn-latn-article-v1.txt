Boshonter moddhe prokolpoti toiri hobe ki na, kingba restorati prothom shit parr korte parbe ki na, keu bolte parto na. Dbiper hate kagoje ekti nirapod hatol eshechhilo, kath ba ispate ekhono noe. Joruri redior ghori tokhono somoe harat, ar bikolpo jenaretorer tokhono natun betari dorkar chhilo. Marar chukti trish november shesh holo. Jaoar age she nothighorer kajer suchi duti draibhe kopi korlo, ekti kopi juner jonno chhapol, ar chhoto karde jachaijer tarikh likhlo. Duti kopi lain dhore meeleeye dekha holo. Sakshatkarogulor kono bektigoto kopi Mara rakhlo na. Protiti boktar pochhondo rekordingjer sange thaklo, ta chalano kompiutarer sange noe.

Shesh shokale Mara eka uttorer chatale gelo. Tokhon bhata, ar purono dhapgulo panir kachh porjonto dekha jachchhilo. Kadar ekti fak perote keu chhoto tokta pete tar ek matha holud rung korechhe. Toktati onumodito meramot chhilo na. Shabdhane bagg hate ekjon manush parr houar moto chooda osthai shetu chhilo setty. Mara chhobi tullo, obosthan likhe rakhlo, ar shohorer rokshonabekshonokormider jonno naught jog korlo. Gronthagarer dik theke bondorer ghontar shobdo shona jachchhilo, jodio shekhane dori tanar keu chhilo na. Batashe setty ekbar beje uthlo - dhile dhatur shobdo, ja shongketo hote parto, abar kebol purono kono jinisher nodachodao. Nijer khataj Mara duti shombhabonai likhe rakhlo.

Nothighor harano nam firie aneni, batighor abar jbalajoni, kingba dbiper bhobishjoto thik kore dejoni. Tobe jonoshomokkhe proshnogulo tola sohoj korechhe ebong onumaner bhittite uttor deoa kothin korechhe. Noksha, hishaber khata, sakshatkar ar neeyaar nil manchitro ekhon alada alada jaegae chhilo; protitir sange chhilo kothaa theke eshechhe ebong kara dekhte parbe tar nothi. June gronthagare kagojer suchi rakhlen. Laela rekordarer pashe shommotir talika rakhlo. Theo redior bjatarite natun lebel lagalo: "proti mashe porikkha karun." Joh chhobiti "uttorer chatal, unishsho churanobboijer age" lekha khape rakhlo. Abohaua thik thakle feri tokhono shatta doshe parr hoto.
