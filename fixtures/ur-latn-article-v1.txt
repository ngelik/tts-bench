Naqsha, ledger, interviews aur Nia ka neela naqsha ab alag alag jagahon par the; har ek ke saath yeh record tha ke woh kahan se aaya aur use kaun dekh sakta hai. June ne kutub khane mein kaaghazi index rakha. Leila ne recorder ke paas razamandi ki list rakhi. Theo ne radio battery par naya label lagaya: "Har mahine jaancho." Jo ne tasveer ko sleeve mein rakh kar likha: "Shumaali chabootra, unnees sau chauraanve se pehle." Mausam ijazat de to feri ab bhi saat baj kar das minute par guzarti thi. Aur jab ghanti do martaba bajti, log oonchi zameen ki taraf dekhte, phir ek doosre ki taraf, yeh dekhne ke liye ke ghanti unhon ne bhi suni hai.
